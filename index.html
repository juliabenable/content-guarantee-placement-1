What creators see now

After picks in the web store, the creator lands back in the app on the chooser. "Free order" opens the card page. A creator who already has a card sees it preselected, with Continue at the bottom.

Meets the 100% rule: No Phyla coverage: 60%
  1. 1Three "finished" signals before any ask: "All done", the big check, "Picks confirmed!".
  2. 2The guarantee is a footnote. It names a thing they don't know and never says "card". People tap the button 3 seconds after landing.
  3. 3A fourth "done": "Your picks are in".
  4. 4It contradicts screen 1. The store said Phyla will place the order. Now we ask how they want to place it. This is the "what do you mean?" reply you get by text.
  5. 5Two menu rows and no button. Pay now got zero real taps. The only price on screen sits on the option nobody picks.
  6. 6The progress bar says Content is next. One creator tapped "Content" twice looking for the next step.
  7. 7A fifth name ("Credit or debit card") and "all creators are required" right after a screen that offered a choice.
  8. 8A card typed by hand on a phone. Several creators left the app mid-form to find their card. One was declined twice by Stripe and had no way forward.
  9. 9Back is an easy exit. It returns to a page that says the order is placed. Eight creators hit Back or Back to Delivery mid-form.
  10. 10A saved card still needs a Continue at the bottom of an empty page. The production copy also has a long dash.
Option A · Proposed · Julia

Fix the flow we have

The card stays after picks, but every screen before it says one thing: you're not done yet. The store stops saying "All done", and the app's Delivery page asks for the card directly, with no chooser.

Meets the 100% rule: No Ship: web today, app next build

The Content Guarantee card

One card on the Delivery page holds the whole ask: what it is, that it's $0 today, and one button. It changes state instead of the page changing around it.

What it fixes

  • No screen says "done" until the card is in. The store sheet is web, so that part can ship today with no app release.
  • One name everywhere: Content Guarantee. No "place the order", no "Free order".
  • One card on Delivery holds the whole ask: status, the $0 line, one button. It shows the saved card when there is one.
  • Saved card: one button that says what it does, "Use this card".

What it doesn't fix

  • The order still ships before the card. Tony's rule needs the card before the brand is told the campaign is full, so A alone doesn't meet it.
  • The only lever would be holding the order until the card is in. That's an engineering change, and the brand still counts the creator as accepted.
  • Still relies on text follow-ups for whoever leaves the page without saving.
Option B · Proposed · Julia · recommended

The card is the commit

"Accept & Commit" already exists. Tapping it opens one sheet: what you get, what you promise, and the card. No card, no acceptance. After that, picks really are the end, and Delivery just shows the order.

Meets the 100% rule: Yes Ship: Monday if Nathan agrees

Why here

  • It's the peak of wanting it: the product is on screen, the brand chose them.
  • The group is still about 20 per campaign, so your texts keep working (they bring back a third to two thirds today).
  • Consent stays tied to one collab, which keeps Katie's legal question simple.
  • It reaches every creator, including everyone onboarded before Monday.

Costs and open bits

  • Accept rate drops, as Tony expects (his example: 55% to 35%). The brand counts a spot only once the card is in.
  • Products aren't picked yet, so the amount is "up to $55". We need a max product value per campaign.
  • Build: a card check on Accept, the existing card form moved into this sheet, the post-pick window and chooser removed. Nathan and Nisarg to size.
Option C · Proposed · Tony and Logan's lean, mocked by Julia

One card for every collab

A new Brand Collabs onboarding step: add a card once, before you're matched to brands. Later, accepting a collab is one tap with the card already on file.

Meets the 100% rule: Yes, with B's gate Ship: after B

What it gets right

  • Brands only ever see creators who already have a card, which is Tony's main point.
  • Accepting a collab is one tap. No money talk at the exciting moment.
  • Framed as joining a network: "this is how gifted collabs work here".

What it costs

  • It's the coldest moment to ask: no brand, no product, no amount.
  • It can't reach anyone already onboarded. They need B's gate anyway (C5).
  • We chase 100 to 150 invitees instead of 20, with a weaker hook ("add a card" vs "claim your serum").
  • The skip link needs a rule: are card-less creators still matched? Tony's call. If there's no opt-out, the link goes.
